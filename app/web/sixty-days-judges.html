The Taskmaster

A deadline keeper, not an appeal decider.

This deployed workflow reads a synthetic decision letter, derives the deadline, routes supporting-document requirements, checks observable photo framing, prepares applicant-controlled requests, and renders an optional draft packet.

Live proof: 23/23 public acceptance checks, 20/20 recorded letter fields, 6/6 recorded evidence checks, and the shared 10/10 exit test.

What is real

Four letters

Four genuine recorded Gemini 3.5 Flash calls are graded against adjacent truth. Applicant names and FEMA registration references are redacted.

Evidence and packet

Observable framing checks return retake, manual review, or ready for applicant review. The PDF is explicitly not submitted.

Durable clock

Eight deterministic wakes cover the deadline. Due actions are recorded idempotently; no insurer, agency, or other third party is contacted.

Safety and claim boundaries

  • Synthetic, unbranded fixtures only; no real survivor data.
  • No legal advice, eligibility prediction, autonomous sending, or submission endpoint.
  • FEMA says an appeal letter or form can be optional; the PDF is an organizing draft.
  • Provisioned service-account roles are not described as per-agent runtime identities.
